Legal framework — how I protect you

Copyright Act 1957

Your content is your IP. Every agreement clearly defines what the brand can use, on which platforms, for how long, and in which territories. Brands using your content in paid ads need explicit ad-usage rights — we charge a premium for that.

ASCI Guidelines

Every campaign includes a disclosure brief. I ensure all content meets the mandatory #Ad, #Sponsored, or #PaidPartnership requirements so you are never exposed to a non-compliance notice. I share the official ASCI guidelines document with you when you join.

Model Release

Your face and voice are yours. Model release clauses in every contract define exactly where your likeness can be used — and for how long. No brand can use your image beyond what is signed.

Payment Terms

50% advance before creation begins. Remaining 50% within 7 days of brand approval. I chase late payments on your behalf. All transactions documented by invoice.

Non-Circumvention

Protects both of us. Brands I introduce cannot bypass me to deal with you directly for 12 months. This is standard agency practice and keeps the relationship honest on all sides.

How a typical deal works

01

Brand brief received

I receive a campaign brief from the brand and assess whether it fits your niche, values, and schedule.

02

Your approval first

I share the brief and brand with you. You review and decide — yes, no, or negotiate. Nothing proceeds without your green light.

03

Agreement signed & advance collected

I finalise the contract, clarify usage rights, and collect 50% advance from the brand before you start.

04

You create, I manage the process

You shoot and deliver the content to me. I handle brand communication, revision feedback, and approval — keeping it clean and stress-free for you.

05

Brand approves, you get paid

Final payment collected and transferred to you within specified days of approval. Every deal closed with a proper invoice trail.
